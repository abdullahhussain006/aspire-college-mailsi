Comprehensive Testing System Send-ups & Pre-Boards

Continuous Assessment, Monthly Tests, Send-up & Pre-Board Examinations

Aspire College Mailsi operates a systematic, three-tier academic testing framework structured to guarantee concept mastery and top board positions:

1. Monthly Unit Tests & Assessment Series: Begins one month after academic classes commence, testing students on weekly modules and chapter units to identify and resolve learning gaps early.
2. Comprehensive Send-up Examinations: Conducted midway and near course completion covering major syllabus portions. Send-ups evaluate cumulative understanding, paper-presentation skills, and exam time management.
3. Pre-Board Grand Exam Series: Full-syllabus mock simulations held prior to final board examinations. Papers strictly emulate board difficulty, question styling, and grading rubrics, training students to perform under authentic board exam conditions.
Parent-Teacher Interaction Student Progress Tracking

Monthly Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTM)

Aspire College Mailsi conducts regular Monthly Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTM) to strengthen direct interaction between respected parents and teachers. Active teacher-parent collaboration ensures proactive support for every student's learning journey and character building.

• Direct Teacher-Parent Collaboration: Parents meet directly with individual subject professors to review monthly test marks, answer sheets, and class participation.
• Attendance & Discipline Monitoring: Transparent review of student attendance records, punctuality, and overall campus conduct.
• Targeted Mentorship Strategy: Faculty and parents collaboratively design customized study routines and remedial guidance for subjects needing attention.
• Open Communication: An encouraging platform where parents share constructive feedback and work hand-in-hand with college leadership for their child's bright future.
