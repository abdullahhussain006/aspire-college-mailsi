Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common queries raised by prospective students and parents.

Ahmad Campus is conveniently situated at Syphon Road, near Mailsi, Garrison, Mailsi, 61200, Pakistan. Our Plus Code on Google Maps is Q5JG+QH Mailsi, Pakistan. The campus is readily accessible via public and private transport with wide roads and dedicated parking.

For F.Sc Pre-Medical and F.Sc Pre-Engineering, candidates must have secured at least 60% marks in Matriculation (Science group with Biology or Computer/Mathematics) from BISE Multan or any recognized Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education in Pakistan.

Under the Syed Omer Nazar Shah Scholarship Program, Aspire Ahmad Campus grants:
• 100% full tuition waiver for students scoring 95%+ in Matric.
• 75% waiver for 90% - 94% marks.
• 50% waiver for 80% - 89% marks.
• 100% free education for orphans.
• 30% kinship discount for siblings and 50% concession for teacher’s children.

Yes, Aspire Group of Colleges Mailsi (Ahmad Campus) is fully recognized and affiliated with the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE) Multan for all intermediate examinations. Our BS programs are affiliated with reputable HEC-recognized universities.

Yes! We operate safe, monitored college buses and vans across all major city routes in Mailsi, Garrison, Syphon Road, Tibba Sultan Pur, Mitroo, and surrounding rural vicinities, ensuring prompt and safe pickup and drop-off for male and female students.

Parents receive regular SMS alerts regarding daily attendance and monthly test scores. Additionally, parents can log into the Aspire Student Portal or attend our bi-monthly Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs) to review performance cards directly with subject specialists.
