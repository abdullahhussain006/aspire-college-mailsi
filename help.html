Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common queries raised by prospective students and parents.

Aspire College Mailsi Campus is conveniently situated at Syphon Road, near Mailsi, Garrison, Mailsi, Punjab, Pakistan. View our official verified location on Google Maps: ASPIRE COLLEGE MAILSI (Q5JG+QH). The campus is readily accessible via public and private transport with wide roads and dedicated parking.

For F.Sc Pre-Medical and F.Sc Pre-Engineering, candidates must have secured at least 60% marks in Matriculation (Science group with Biology or Computer/Mathematics) from BISE Multan or any recognized Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education in Pakistan.

Under Our Scholarship Program, Aspire College grants:
• 100% full tuition waiver for students scoring 95%+ in Matric.
• 75% waiver for 90% - 94% marks.
• 50% waiver for 80% - 89% marks.
• 100% free education for orphans.
• 30% kinship discount for siblings and 50% concession for teacher’s children.

Yes, Aspire Group of Colleges Mailsi is fully recognized and affiliated with the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE) Multan for all intermediate examinations. Our BS programs are affiliated with reputable HEC-recognized universities.

Yes! We operate safe, monitored college buses and vans across all major city routes in Mailsi, Garrison, Syphon Road and surrounding rural vicinities around 20 Km, ensuring prompt and safe pickup and drop-off for male and female students.

Parents receive regular SMS alerts regarding daily attendance and monthly test scores. Additionally, parents can log into the Aspire Guardian App to get their Son/Daughter Academic Report. Also we conduct Parents Teacher Meeting every month.
