Academic Streams & Disciplines

Empowering students with structured curricula affiliated with the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education (BISE) Multan and renowned universities.

Intermediate Level

F.Sc Pre-Medical

Designed for future medical professionals, dentists, pharmacists, and biotechnologists. At Aspire College Mailsi, Pre-Medical students receive in-depth conceptual instruction in biological and physical sciences, paired with dedicated MDCAT foundation classes.

Core Electives:
Biology Chemistry Physics English Compulsory Urdu Compulsory Islamic Studies / Pak Studies
Duration: 2 Years
Affiliation: BISE Multan
Min Marks: 60% in Matric
Intermediate Level

F.Sc Pre-Engineering

The premier launchpad for engineering, architecture, aviation, and technological disciplines. Focuses on advanced mathematics, Newtonian & modern physics, and chemical sciences, preparing students for ECAT, UET, NUST, FAST, and GIKI.

Core Electives:
Mathematics Physics Chemistry English Compulsory Urdu Compulsory Pak Studies / Islamiyat
Duration: 2 Years
Affiliation: BISE Multan
Min Marks: 60% in Matric
Computing & IT

ICS (Intermediate in Computer Science)

Equips students with foundational programming principles, computer architecture, algorithmic reasoning, and applied mathematics for high-growth tech careers in Software Engineering, AI, and Cybersecurity.

Available Combinations:
Computer + Math + Physics Computer + Math + Statistics Computer + Math + Economics
Duration: 2 Years
Affiliation: BISE Multan
Min Marks: 50% in Matric
Business & Finance

I.Com (Intermediate in Commerce)

Provides practical training in financial accounting, banking systems, mercantile law, commercial geography, and business math. The prime starting route for CA, ACCA, BBA, and banking sectors.

Core Electives:
Principles of Accounting Principles of Commerce Principles of Economics Business Math & Stats Commercial Geography & Banking
Duration: 2 Years
Affiliation: BISE Multan
Min Marks: 45% in Matric
