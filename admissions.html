Empowerment Policy

Our Scholarship Schemes

Aspire firmly believes financial constraints should never extinguish the flame of talent. We allocate millions annually in fee concessions:

100% Full Tuition Waiver
For students scoring 95%+ in BISE Multan matriculation exams.
75% & 50% High-Merit Concessions
For students with 90%+ and 80%+ marks respectively.
Special Humanitarian Concessions
100% free education for orphans, 30% for siblings, and 50% for teacher's children.
Procedure

How to Secure Your Admission

A simple 4-step streamlined process from online registration to classroom orientation.

1

Submit Application

Fill out the online application below or visit Aspire College on Syphon Road Mailsi.

2

Academic Counseling

Meet our senior career counselor with your parents to finalize your best subject stream.

3

Document Verification

Submit Matric result card/roll no slip, 4 photos, B-Form & father's CNIC copy.

4

Fee Voucher & ID

Deposit fee with approved scholarship concession and collect your student ID & uniform slip.
