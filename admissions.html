Empowerment Policy

Our Scholarship Schemes

Aspire believes financial constraints should never limit a student’s potential. We offer fee concessions to support deserving students and make quality education more accessible.

100% Full Tuition Waiver
For top position holders and high achievers in board examinations.
50% High-Merit Concessions
For students with 90%+ marks in examination
Special Humanitarian Concessions
100% free education for orphans, 25% for siblings and teacher's children.
Procedure

How to Secure Your Admission

A simple 4-step streamlined process from online registration to classroom orientation.

1

Submit Application

Fill out the online application below or visit Aspire College Mailsi.

2

Academic Counseling

Meet our senior career counselor with your parents to finalize your best subject stream.

3

Document Verification

Submit Matric result card/roll no slip, 4 photos, B-Form & father's CNIC copy.

4

Fee Voucher & ID

Deposit fee with approved scholarship concession and collect your student ID & uniform slip.
