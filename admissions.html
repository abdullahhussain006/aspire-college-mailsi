Empowerment Policy

Our Scholarship Schemes

Aspire firmly believes financial constraints should never extinguish the flame of talent. We allocate millions annually in fee concessions:

100% Full Tuition Waiver
For students scoring 95%+ in BISE Multan matriculation exams.
75% & 50% High-Merit Concessions
For students with 90%+ and 80%+ marks respectively.
Special Humanitarian Concessions
100% free education for orphans, 30% for siblings, and 50% for teacher's children.
